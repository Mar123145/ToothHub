Clinic Policies

Appointment Fee

  • A fixed appointment fee of ₱ is required to submit a booking request.
  • The appointment fee is non-refundable and not deductible from the final treatment cost.
  • Your booking request will be reviewed after receipt verification.

Pricing & Treatment

  • Prices shown are estimates only. Final cost may change after assessment and actual treatment.
  • Some procedures may require additional steps based on the dentist’s evaluation.

Reschedule

  • Reschedule requests must be made at least 24 hours before the appointment time.
  • Each appointment may be rescheduled up to 2 times only.
  • Reschedule is subject to dentist availability.

Cancellation & No-show

  • Valid cancellation: If you cancel at least 24 hours before your scheduled time, your cancellation is valid.
  • Refund eligibility: For valid cancellations (24+ hours), the appointment fee may be refunded, after verification.
  • Late cancellation / no-show: Cancellations within 24 hours of the appointment time or no-shows may result in forfeiture of the appointment fee.
  • Repeated no-shows may restrict future booking requests.

Receipt Verification

  • Please upload a clear receipt. If the receipt is unclear/invalid, the clinic may request a re-upload.
  • If a re-upload request is not complied with within 24 hours, the booking request may be declined.

Clinic-Initiated Changes

  • The clinic may reschedule in emergencies (e.g., dentist illness or equipment issues). You will be notified as soon as possible.
