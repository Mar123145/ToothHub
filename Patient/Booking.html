Clinic Policies

Appointment Fee

  • A fixed appointment fee of ₱ is required to submit a booking request.
  • The appointment fee is generally non-refundable and not deductible from the final treatment cost.
  • If the clinic marks an appointment as unavailable, the patient will be notified and may choose to request a reschedule or a cancellation.
  • Your booking request will be reviewed after receipt verification.

Pricing & Treatment

  • Prices shown are estimates only. Final cost may change after assessment and actual treatment.
  • Optional add-ons may be requested by the patient and discussed during treatment based on the dentist’s evaluation. Pricing may vary accordingly and will proceed only upon the patient’s agreement.

Reschedule

  • Reschedule requests are only available after appointment approval.
  • Reschedule requests must be made at least 24 hours before the appointment time.
  • Each appointment may be rescheduled up to 2 times only.
  • Reschedule is subject to dentist availability.

Cancellation & No-show

  • Valid cancellation: If you cancel at least 24 hours before your scheduled time, your cancellation is valid.
  • Refund eligibility: The appointment fee may be refunded or voided after verification if:
    • the patient cancels at least 24 hours before the appointment time, or
    • the clinic marks the appointment as unavailable, and the patient chooses to cancel instead of rescheduling.
  • Late cancellation / no-show: Cancellations within 24 hours of the appointment time or no-shows may result in forfeiture of the appointment fee.
  • Repeated no-shows may restrict future booking requests.

Receipt Verification

  • Please upload a clear receipt. If the receipt is unclear/invalid, the clinic may request a re-upload.
  • If a re-upload request is not complied with within 24 hours, the booking request may be declined.

Clinic-Initiated Changes

  • The clinic may mark an appointment as unavailable due to unforeseen circumstances (e.g., dentist illness, emergency closure, or equipment issues). The patient will be notified and may choose to request a reschedule or cancellation.
